# Music

Ambient soundtrack while your agents work.

AgentCraft includes an ambient soundtrack to set the mood while your agents work. Toggle it on or off from the UI.

## Enabling Music

Click the music toggle in the top bar to start playback. Use the volume slider to adjust the level.

## How It Works

- Sequential playlist — Tracks play in order, looping back to the start when the playlist ends
- Silence gaps — Brief pauses between tracks for a natural listening experience
- Audio ducking — Music volume automatically lowers when notification sounds play
- Cross-tab coordination — Only one tab plays music at a time to prevent overlapping audio

## Controls

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Music toggle | Start/stop playback |
| Volume slider | Adjust music volume |
